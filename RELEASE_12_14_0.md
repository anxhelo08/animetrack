# AnimeTrack 12.14.0 — Season Focus & Personal Timeline

- Opening a title now centers the horizontal season row on the resume season and marks it with “KU E LE”.
- Resume ignores hidden parts and still opens the page containing the next/last watched episode.
- Any season/movie/OVA/special can be hidden from the personal timeline without deleting progress. Hidden parts can be restored from “Pjesë të fshehura”.
- Hidden parts do not count toward watched totals, progress percentage, next episode, season numbering, upcoming-season alerts or season-completion prompts.
- Season descriptions are stored per part. AniList provides anime/movie descriptions; MyAnimeList is a fallback; TVMaze show summaries describe TV franchise seasons.
- Hidden choices survive franchise refreshes and cloud sync.
