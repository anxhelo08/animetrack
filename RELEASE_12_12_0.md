# AnimeTrack 12.12.0 — Franchise Timeline

- Anime franchise timeline now follows official AniList PREQUEL/SEQUEL relations across TV, ONA, OVA, movies and specials.
- Parts are sorted by first release date and labelled independently: Season 1, Film, Season 2, etc.
- Separately-added linked entries are reconciled into one library card while watched progress/history is preserved.
- Replaced the confusing anime “Ndarja si serial (TV)” action with a single “Përditëso serinë” action.
- Movie entries get a safe one-item timeline row so they can be marked watched without pretending to be a TV season.
- TV-series families with colon-based sequel titles are grouped generically (Dexter is no longer hard-coded), sorted by premiere year, and can be refreshed from TVMaze.
